# Evidence verification

- Knowledge ID: `trade.evidence-verification`
- Knowledge version: `1.0.0`
- Status: active analytical guidance
- Applies to: external evidence used in Trade Engine research
- Authority: cited primary sources and exact data-provider methodology/version

## Evidence record

For each material claim, retain:

- exact claim and whether it is fact, deterministic calculation, or inference;
- publisher, title, stable URL/identifier, and source type;
- publication/dissemination time, event/effective time, covered period, and retrieval as-of time;
- units, currency, adjustments, restatement/revision status, and applicable entity/instrument;
- support, contradiction, limitations, and confidence based on evidence quality.

## Source priority

Prefer, in order: legally authoritative records and official statistics; exchange/regulator/issuer primary material; first-party provider methodology and exact datasets; peer-reviewed or original research; reputable secondary reporting. A secondary article that repeats a filing is not independent corroboration.

## Point-in-time rule

Historical relevance depends on the earliest time the exact information was publicly available, not the event date or current page content. Preserve prior filing and dataset vintages when conclusions concern a historical decision. Treat silently updated pages, undated downloads, revised macro series, adjusted constituents, and current fundamentals as unresolved until availability is proven.

## Challenge pattern

Ask one useful counter-question: what different mechanism explains the same observation, and what new evidence separates the mechanisms? Check base rates, selection effects, unit/definition differences, common upstream sources, incentives, and whether price already incorporated the information.

Stop when the claim is adequately supported for the decision, clearly refuted, or blocked by a named missing source. More sources are not automatically better.
